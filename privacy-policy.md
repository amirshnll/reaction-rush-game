# Privacy Policy for Reaction Rush

Reaction Rush does not collect personal information, browsing history, website content, location data, or advertising identifiers. The game runs locally within the extension popup and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking. Game data is not sent to a server, sold, or shared with third parties.

## Data Stored

The extension stores language and sound preferences, together with local statistics including games played, highest score, fastest reaction, total reaction time, and successful clicks. This data is used only to display the user's records and average performance.

## Why the `storage` Permission Is Required

The `storage` permission is used only to preserve game settings and statistics between sessions. It is not used to read websites, browsing history, device files, or account information, and it does not transmit data over the internet.

## External Link

The donation link opens an external website only when selected by the user. The privacy policy of that website applies after the user leaves the extension.
